# Tooltip templates and localized labels

These supplementary source entries include labels and templates filled by the game. Numbered placeholders are arguments; inventory, rates, costs, and requirements are supplied at runtime. Literal templates are retained below.

## unlock_tooltip_template_cost

```text
### Unlock Cost {{{{itemblock cost unlock {0}}}}}
```
